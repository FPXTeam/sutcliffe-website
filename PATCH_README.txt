Sutcliffe Trading - Building & Construction Carousel Patch

WHAT THIS PATCH CHANGES
- Replaces the two-image Building & Construction gallery on /timber with the existing ProductCarousel component.
- Adds 9 enhanced Building & Construction product images.
- Does not change Manufacturing, Outdoor & Landscaping, Dunnage, homepage, navigation, contact form, SEO metadata, or other site files.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the included app/ and public/ folders into the ROOT of your existing Sutcliffe Trading repo.
3. Allow app/timber/page.tsx to replace the existing file.
4. The new image folder is added at public/images/product-groups/building-construction/carousel/.

IMPORTANT
The older Building & Construction image files are intentionally not deleted because they may still be referenced elsewhere. They simply stop rendering in the /timber Building & Construction section.
